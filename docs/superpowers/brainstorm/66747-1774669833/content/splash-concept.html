What kind of splash screen?

Replacing the landing page at / — which concept fits Ordio?

A

Branded Gate

Full-screen branded moment — logo, tagline, single CTA button ("Start creating"). No marketing copy. Just entry. Think: Notion dark splash or Linear login.

B

Auth-First Entry

Splash is essentially the sign-in/sign-up screen. The Clerk auth UI lives on the home page directly. Logged-in users are immediately redirected to /create. No extra screen.

C

Animated Intro

Brief animated waveform/orb moment on first load, then transitions into the create page. Like an app launch screen. Dismisses automatically or on tap.

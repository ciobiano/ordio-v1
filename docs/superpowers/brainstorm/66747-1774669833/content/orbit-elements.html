What floats around the center?

Luma uses 3D event objects. For Ordio, what orbits the core?

A

Audio tools

Mic, waveform bar clusters, headphones, a caption bubble, a share icon. Represents the record → transcribe → export flow.

B

Audiogram previews

Mini floating cards showing sample audiograms — a waveform style, a caption style, a format. Like a product showcase orbiting the center.

C

Pure waveform geometry

Abstract — only waveform fragments, rings, and particles. No literal icons. More editorial, less illustrative.
